Privacy Policy

Learn what personal information ASPAS Office collects, why it is used, who may receive it and how to make a privacy request.

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how ASPAS Office, operated as a sole proprietorship under the name ASPAS Office, handles personal data when you use aspasoffice.com, contact us, submit an enquiry, or use our services. “Personal data” means information that identifies or relates to an individual.

2. Personal data we may collect

  • Enquiry details: name, phone number, email address, preferred city or location, service or plan selected, and information you enter in a message.
  • Service and verification details: business name, entity and registration details, billing information, and identity, address or supporting documents where they are requested for a service, partner verification or legal compliance.
  • Communications and transactions: messages with us, quotations, orders, payment status and support history. Payment card or bank credentials should be entered only through the payment provider’s secure page, if one is used; we do not ask you to send passwords or one-time codes by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: information automatically available to the website host or service providers, such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages and timestamps, for delivery, security and troubleshooting.
  • Preferences: a small browser storage entry may remember your selected display theme. It is stored on your device and is not used to identify you.

Please do not send sensitive documents until we explain why they are needed and how to provide them securely.

3. How we use data

We use personal data to respond to questions; assess and arrange requested services; check location or partner availability; coordinate onboarding and verification; manage payments, renewals and support; maintain website security; meet legal obligations; resolve disputes; and communicate material service or policy changes. We use information for these purposes only as permitted by applicable law and, where required, with your consent.

4. When information is shared

We share only the information needed for the relevant purpose. This may include the coworking or address partner you select, for KYC checks and preparing the partner documents for your service; Google Apps Script and email providers that handle website enquiries; hosting and technical providers; payment providers if a payment is processed; professional advisers; and public authorities where required or legally permitted. We do not sell personal data.

When you submit the website enquiry form, Google Apps Script processes the submitted details and routes the enquiry to ASPAS Office for follow-up. The website also loads its logo from Cloudinary and fonts from Google Fonts; those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser loads their resources.

Official KYC and business documents are collected by email, not through WhatsApp. We retain copies in ASPAS Office email records for onboarding, service administration, security review, handling suspected misuse of the service and meeting legal or accounting obligations. The selected partner may keep copies in its email or records for KYC, preparing partner documents and its own legal or operational obligations. We limit sharing to what the requested service requires and periodically review whether retained copies are still needed. Ask the partner directly about its retention practices.

5. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, the service relationship, applicable record-keeping requirements, or the establishment or defence of legal claims. We periodically review information and delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed, subject to lawful retention requirements.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and service, and limit document access to people who need it for the service. Email is not end-to-end encrypted in every situation; when sending especially sensitive attachments, ask us for a secure method or use a password-protected file and share its password through a separate channel. Never send passwords, one-time codes or full payment credentials.

Use WhatsApp for ordinary coordination only; do not send official documents or KYC attachments there. You are responsible for securing your own phone and messaging account. ASPAS Office cannot control exposure caused solely by unauthorized access to your device or account, but this statement does not limit ASPAS Office’s responsibility for its own handling of data or any rights you have under law. If you accidentally send a document through WhatsApp, notify us promptly and we will take reasonable steps to delete copies under our control.

6. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to confirm whether we hold your personal data, provide access to it, correct or update inaccurate information, erase information that we no longer need or are not legally required to keep, or withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. You may also raise a concern or complaint. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully, and some data may need to be retained to provide an active service or meet legal obligations.

Email legal@aspasoffice.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. Tell us what you need and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant enquiry or service. We may verify your identity before acting. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

7. Cookies and external links

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies. It may use browser storage to remember your theme preference, and the hosting or security provider may use essential technologies to deliver and protect the site. External links and embedded third-party services are governed by their own privacy notices; review those notices before using them.

8. Children

Our website and business services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly seek personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
