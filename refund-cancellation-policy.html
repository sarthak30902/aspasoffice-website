Refund & Cancellation Policy

Understand how cancellation and refund requests are reviewed for ASPAS Office services, including virtual office and business address arrangements.

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Read your order confirmation

This policy applies to services booked through ASPAS Office. Before payment, your quotation or order confirmation should state the selected service, location, included documents or facilities, price and taxes, start or activation date, duration, renewal terms, any deposit, and any known partner or third-party charges. If an order-specific term differs from this page, the order-specific term applies to that service to the extent permitted by law.

Please confirm the written scope before paying. An enquiry or availability check is not a booking. We will not treat an uncommunicated fee as accepted; material extra charges must be disclosed and approved before they are incurred, except amounts charged directly by a third party.

2. How to request a cancellation or refund

Email legal@aspasoffice.com with the subject “Cancellation and Refund Request”. Include your name, order or invoice reference, service, location, payment date and reason. You may also contact connect@aspasoffice.com. We will acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days; any mandatory legal deadline takes precedence. A cancellation request does not affect any statutory consumer right.

3. Refund cases

  1. Cancellation before documentation work starts — full refund. If you have paid and request a refund before ASPAS Office or its partner has started the documentation work for your order, we will provide a full refund regardless of the reason for cancellation.
  2. Cancellation after work starts — 20% deduction. If documentation work has started and the request is not covered by the specific rejection case below, 20% of the total amount paid for the affected service will be retained and the remaining 80% refunded. This deduction is applied once and is not stacked with the 10% rejection deduction.
  3. Service purpose not fulfilled because an application was rejected:
    • Error attributable to ASPAS Office: If the rejection resulted from a document mistake or mismatch caused by ASPAS Office, 10% of the total amount paid for the affected service will be retained and the remaining 90% refunded.
    • Error attributable to the client: If the rejection resulted solely from inaccurate, incomplete, inconsistent or late information or documents supplied by the client, or from the client not following requirements communicated before submission, no refund will be provided under this case.
    • Other or shared causes: If the rejection resulted from a partner, authority requirement or change, or causes not clearly attributable to either party, ASPAS Office will review the service delivered and offer an appropriate remedy, which may include an alternative service or a proportionate refund.

We will explain the basis for a rejection decision and any calculation in writing. If the cause is disputed, we will review the relevant order, submitted documents and communications before deciding. These cases do not limit any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded.

4. Other refund situations

  • ASPAS Office or partner cannot provide the confirmed service before activation: You may choose an available alternative. If you do not accept it, we will refund the amount paid for the unavailable service in full.
  • Partner service ends after activation: We will review the unused portion of the service and offer a proportionate refund or suitable alternative, subject to the order and applicable law.
  • Duplicate payment: A verified duplicate payment will be refunded in full to the original payment method.
  • Authority delay or rejection not caused by a document error: An authority makes its own decision, and a delay or rejection alone does not mean ASPAS Office failed to provide the agreed documentation or address service. We will still review whether the confirmed service was delivered as promised and whether a remedy is due.

5. Refund processing

We aim to review a complete request and share a decision within 10 business days. Once approved, we will initiate the refund to the original payment method within 7 business days. Banks and payment providers may take additional time to post it. Where tax adjustments are required, we will process them as applicable law requires.

6. Escalation

If you disagree with a decision, reply to the decision email with your order reference and the reason for reconsideration. ASPAS Office’s legal team will review the complaint. You may also use any statutory consumer remedy available to you.
