Refund & Cancellation Policy

Understand how cancellation and refund requests are reviewed for ASPAS Office services, including virtual office and business address arrangements.

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Read your order confirmation

This policy applies to services booked through ASPAS Office. Before payment, your quotation or order confirmation should state the selected service, location, included documents or facilities, price and taxes, start or activation date, duration, renewal terms, any deposit, and any known partner or third-party charges. If an order-specific term differs from this page, the order-specific term applies to that service to the extent permitted by law.

Please confirm the written scope before paying. An enquiry or availability check is not a booking. We will not treat an uncommunicated fee as accepted; material extra charges must be disclosed and approved before they are incurred, except amounts charged directly by a third party.

2. When you may cancel

To cancel, email legal@aspasoffice.com using the subject “Cancellation and Refund Request”. Include your name, order or invoice reference, location, service, payment date and reason. You may also contact connect@aspasoffice.com. We will confirm receipt and tell you if more information is needed. A cancellation takes effect when we confirm it in writing; any statutory cancellation right applies as provided by law.

3. Refund eligibility

  • Before onboarding or work begins: If you cancel before ASPAS Office or its partner has begun the confirmed work, we will refund amounts paid, less only reasonable, documented, non-recoverable third-party costs that were disclosed to you before payment and are permitted by law.
  • After onboarding or partner work has begun: We will assess the unperformed portion of the service, work already completed, and any documented third-party commitments or charges that were disclosed before payment. Any amount retained will be proportionate and permitted by law. We will explain the calculation in writing.
  • Location or service unavailable before activation: If ASPAS Office or its partner cannot provide the confirmed service at the agreed location before activation, we will offer an available alternative. If you do not accept it, we will refund the amount paid for the unavailable service, subject to applicable law.
  • Duplicate or mistaken payment: A verified duplicate payment will be returned in full to the original payment method, subject to payment-provider checks.
  • Authority decision: A government authority’s rejection or delay does not by itself mean the address or documentation service was not provided. However, if the confirmed service was materially not delivered as promised, contact us so we can review the case and any available remedy.

4. Services already activated or used

Once a service has been activated or used, charges for the completed period or work may not be refundable to the extent they were clearly disclosed, actually provided and permitted by law. If a service ends early because ASPAS Office or its partner cannot continue providing it, we will assess a proportionate refund or other remedy for the unused portion, according to the order and applicable law. No term in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

5. Meeting-room and other one-time bookings

Any cancellation cutoff, rescheduling option or no-show charge for a meeting room, courier, document or other one-time service must be stated in the booking confirmation before payment. If no special cutoff was provided, contact us as soon as possible; we will review the request based on whether the service or partner commitment has already been provided or incurred.

6. Refund processing

We aim to review a complete refund request and share a decision within 10 business days. If approved, we will initiate the refund to the original payment method within 7 business days. Your bank, card issuer or payment provider may take additional time to post the credit. If a delay occurs because information is missing or a provider is investigating a transaction, we will let you know.

7. Complaints and escalation

If you disagree with a decision, reply to the decision email with the order reference and the reason you believe it should be reconsidered. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days, subject to applicable law and any information needed from you. You may also use any statutory consumer remedy available to you.
